Syntax Highlighting & Formatting

Offline Data URL Conversion

Converts saved images into local SVG and Base64 Data URLs. Even if the hosting website removes or deletes the image, your copy remains preserved offline forever.

Draggable Glassmorphism Floater

Access your history in an un-intrusive floating overlay. Seamlessly drag, resize, or minimize it to fit your exact screen workflow with boundary protection.

Real-Time Instant Search

Find any clip in milliseconds. Filter through up to 5,000 saved items by keyword, file size, or timestamp, and click once to copy it right back.

100% Private & Local Storage

Zero telemetry, zero trackers, and zero external servers. Everything is stored strictly inside your browser's local sandbox with unlimited storage capacity.

JSON Backup & Restore

Easily export your entire history into clean JSON backup files. Import and merge backups across machines with automated deduplication.
